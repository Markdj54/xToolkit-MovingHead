## Design Principle

xToolkit preserves artistic intent.

The application does not generate lighting movements,
paths, timing or choreography.

Its responsibility is limited to remapping DMX channel
references while preserving the original xLights
programming.

Where possible, the original XSQ document is preserved
and only the translated EffectDB entries are modified.

Current investigations indicate that modifying EffectDB entries alone should be sufficient. This will be verified during Sprint 11.
